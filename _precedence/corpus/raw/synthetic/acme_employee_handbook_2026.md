# US EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (EXCERPT) — 2026 EDITION

Applies to all employees of Acme Cloud Systems, Inc. working in the United States.
This handbook is not an employment contract and does not alter the at-will nature of
employment.

## 4. Time Off

### 4.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)

4.1.1 Regular employees accrue PTO each pay period based on hours worked. Hours worked
in excess of forty (40) in a workweek are not counted toward accrual.

4.1.2 The accrual factor is determined by completed years of service:

| Completed years of service | Accrual factor (hours of PTO per hour worked) | Approximate annual accrual at 40 hours/week |
|---|---|---|
| Fewer than 5 | 0.0961 | 200 hours |
| 5 through 9 | 0.1154 | 240 hours |
| 10 or more | 0.1346 | 280 hours |

4.1.3 New employees are advanced forty (40) hours of PTO on their first day of
employment. Advanced PTO is charged against subsequent accrual.

4.1.4 Accrued unused PTO carries over into the following calendar year up to one and
one-half (1.5) times the employee's approximate annual accrual. PTO accrued above that
balance is forfeited on December 31.

4.1.5 PTO must be requested at least five (5) business days in advance for absences of
three (3) or more consecutive days, and requires manager approval.

### 4.2 Paid Sick Leave

4.2.1 Employees accrue one (1) hour of paid sick leave for every forty (40) hours
worked, beginning on the first day of employment.

4.2.2 Accrued paid sick leave may be used beginning on the ninetieth (90th) calendar
day of employment.

4.2.3 An employee may use no more than forty (40) hours of paid sick leave in a
calendar year.

4.2.4 Up to forty (40) hours of accrued unused paid sick leave carries over to the
following calendar year.

4.2.5 Accrued unused paid sick leave is not paid out on separation of employment.

### 4.6 Compliance With State and Local Law

4.6.1 Where a state or local law requires a more generous rate of accrual, a higher
accrual or use cap, a shorter waiting period, or greater carryover than this Section 4
provides, that law applies to employees working in that jurisdiction in place of the
corresponding provision of this handbook.

## 7. Business Expenses

7.1 Approval thresholds for business expenses are set out in the Acme Corporate
Expense Reimbursement Policy. A department may adopt a policy that is more restrictive
than the Corporate Expense Reimbursement Policy. Where a department policy and the
Corporate Expense Reimbursement Policy differ, the more restrictive approval
requirement controls.

7.2 Expense reports must be submitted within sixty (60) days after the expense is
incurred. Reports submitted after that period require Controller approval.

## 9. Separation of Employment

9.1 Employees are asked, but not required, to provide two (2) weeks' written notice of
resignation.

9.2 Company property must be returned on or before the last day of employment.

9.3 Final wages, including any earned and unpaid incentive compensation, are paid on
the next regular payroll date following the employee's last day of employment.

9.4 Accrued unused PTO is not paid out on separation, except where applicable state law
requires payout, in which case it is paid at the employee's final base rate of pay.

9.5 Where applicable state law requires final wages to be paid earlier than the next
regular payroll date, Payroll will issue final wages by the deadline that law requires.
