# EXHIBIT A — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (STANDARD)

## A.1 Definitions

"Monthly Uptime Percentage" means, for a calendar month, the total number of minutes
in that month less the number of minutes of Downtime, divided by the total number of
minutes in that month, expressed as a percentage.

"Monthly Service Fee" means the recurring Fee payable for the affected Service for the
calendar month in which the Downtime occurred, excluding one-time and usage charges.

"Downtime" excludes unavailability caused by (a) scheduled maintenance announced at
least forty-eight (48) hours in advance, (b) Customer's equipment or network, or
(c) a force majeure event under Section 15 of the Agreement.

## A.2 Service Commitment

Acme will make the Services available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least
99.9% during each calendar month.

## A.3 Service Credits (Standard Tier)

If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below the Service Commitment, Customer is
eligible for a Service Credit calculated as a percentage of the Monthly Service Fee:

| Monthly Uptime Percentage | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| Less than 99.9% but equal to or greater than 99.0% | 10% |
| Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0% | 25% |
| Less than 95.0% | 100% |

## A.4 Credit Request Procedure

A.4.1 To receive a Service Credit, Customer must submit a request to Acme support
within thirty (30) days after the end of the calendar month in which the Downtime
occurred. Requests submitted after that period are not eligible.

A.4.2 Service Credits in any calendar month shall not exceed one hundred percent
(100%) of the Monthly Service Fee for the affected Service.

A.4.3 Service Credits are applied against future invoices and are not refundable in
cash. Service Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for Acme's failure to
meet the Service Commitment.
