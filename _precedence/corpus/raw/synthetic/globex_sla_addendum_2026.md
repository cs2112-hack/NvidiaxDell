# SERVICE LEVEL ADDENDUM

This Service Level Addendum is entered into as of June 15, 2026 between Acme Cloud
Systems, Inc. and Globex Retail Group, following the April 2026 platform incident.

## 2. Enhanced Service Credits

2.1 Where the Monthly Uptime Percentage for the Services falls below the Service
Commitment, Customer is eligible for a Service Credit as follows:

| Monthly Uptime Percentage | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| Less than 99.9% but equal to or greater than 99.0% | 20% |
| Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0% | 40% |
| Less than 95.0% | 100% |

2.2 The thirty (30) day credit request window in the Service Level Exhibit continues to
apply.

## 3. Incident Reporting

3.1 Acme shall deliver a written root-cause analysis within five (5) business days
after the close of any incident that causes more than sixty (60) minutes of continuous
Downtime.

## 4. Relationship to the Service Level Exhibit

4.1 This Addendum supplements the Service Level Exhibit. The parties have not agreed an
order of precedence between this Addendum and the Service Level Exhibit, and each
document stands on its own terms.
