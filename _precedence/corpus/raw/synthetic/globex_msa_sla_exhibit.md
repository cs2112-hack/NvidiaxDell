# SERVICE LEVEL EXHIBIT

Attached to and forming part of the Master Services Agreement dated February 1, 2026
between Acme Cloud Systems, Inc. and Globex Retail Group ("Customer").

## 1. Service Commitment

1.1 Acme will make the Services available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least
99.9% during each calendar month. "Monthly Uptime Percentage" and "Monthly Service Fee"
have the meanings given in the Master Services Agreement.

1.2 The Monthly Service Fee for the Services is thirty thousand dollars ($30,000).

## 2. Service Credits

2.1 If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below the Service Commitment, Customer is
eligible for a Service Credit as follows:

| Monthly Uptime Percentage | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| Less than 99.9% but equal to or greater than 99.0% | 10% |
| Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0% | 25% |
| Less than 95.0% | 100% |

2.2 Customer must request a Service Credit within thirty (30) days after the end of the
affected calendar month.
