# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 2

Issued under the Consulting Services Agreement dated September 15, 2025 between
Pinnacle Advisory Partners LLC ("Consultant") and Acme Cloud Systems, Inc. ("Company").

## 1. Services

1.1 Consultant shall provide FedRAMP readiness assessment and remediation advisory
services as described in Attachment 1.

## 2. Period of Performance

2.1 April 1, 2026 through September 30, 2026, unless extended by written change order.

## 3. Fees

3.1 Services are provided on a time-and-materials basis at a blended rate of two
hundred twenty-five dollars ($225.00) per hour.

3.2 The total fees payable under this Statement of Work shall not exceed one hundred
twelve thousand five hundred dollars ($112,500.00), corresponding to five hundred
(500) hours, without a written change order signed by Company.

3.3 Consultant shall notify Company in writing when eighty percent (80%) of the amount
in Section 3.2 has been incurred.

3.4 Hours in excess of forty (40) in any week require advance written approval from
Company's project sponsor and are billed at the same rate.

## 4. Expenses

4.1 Pre-approved travel expenses are reimbursed at cost, without markup, and are
included in the maximum amount stated in Section 3.2.

4.2 Expense documentation must accompany the invoice for the month in which the expense
was incurred.

## 5. Invoicing and Payment

5.1 Consultant shall invoice monthly in arrears, within ten (10) days after the end of
the month, with a detailed statement of hours by person and task.

5.2 Invoices are due net thirty (30) days from receipt. Company may withhold disputed
amounts pending resolution.

5.3 Invoices submitted more than ninety (90) days after the end of the month in which
the services were performed are not payable.

## 6. Liability

6.1 Consultant's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Statement of
Work shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Statement of Work.

6.2 The limitation in Section 6.1 does not apply to Consultant's gross negligence,
willful misconduct, or breach of its confidentiality obligations.
