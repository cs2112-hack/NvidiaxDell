# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") is entered into as of September 1, 2026 between
Acme Cloud Systems, Inc. and Northwind Logistics LLC, and amends the Master Services
Agreement dated January 1, 2026 (the "MSA").

## 1. Insurance

1.1 Acme shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less
than two million dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence and cyber liability insurance
with limits of not less than five million dollars ($5,000,000) in the aggregate, and
shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 2. Limitation of Liability

2.1 Section 9.2 of the MSA is deleted and replaced with the following: "Each party's
aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement, and to any Order Form
issued under it, shall not exceed five hundred thousand dollars ($500,000) for all
claims of every kind."

2.2 Section 9.3 of the MSA (Excluded Claims) is unchanged.

## 3. Effect of Amendment

3.1 Except as expressly amended above, the MSA remains in full force and effect.

3.2 This Amendment and any Order Form issued under the MSA are of equal rank. Neither
this Amendment nor any Order Form shall be deemed to take precedence over the other,
and Section 14.3 of the MSA shall not apply as between this Amendment and any Order
Form.
