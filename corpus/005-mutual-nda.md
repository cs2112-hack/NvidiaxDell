# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## N-1 Parties and purpose

**N-1.1** This Agreement is made between the Company and the Counterparty for the
purpose of permitting each party to evaluate a potential commercial relationship
(the "Permitted Purpose").

**N-1.2** Each party may act as Discloser in respect of information it discloses
and as Recipient in respect of information disclosed to it.

## N-2 Confidential Information

**N-2.1** "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by or on
behalf of the Discloser to the Recipient, whether before or after the date of
this Agreement, whether in writing, orally or by inspection of tangible objects,
which is designated as confidential or which by its nature or the circumstances
of its disclosure ought reasonably to be regarded as confidential.

**N-2.2** Confidential Information includes the existence and contents of this
Agreement and the fact that the parties are in discussions.

**N-2.3** Confidential Information does not include information which:

  (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a
      breach of this Agreement by the Recipient;
  (b) was lawfully in the Recipient's possession without obligation of
      confidence before disclosure by the Discloser;
  (c) is lawfully obtained by the Recipient from a third party who is not under
      an obligation of confidence in respect of it; or
  (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to
      the Confidential Information.

## N-3 Obligations of the Recipient

**N-3.1** The Recipient will keep the Confidential Information confidential and
will not disclose it to any person except as permitted by this Agreement.

**N-3.2** The Recipient will use the Confidential Information solely for the
Permitted Purpose.

**N-3.3** The Recipient will protect the Confidential Information using no less
than the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of like
importance, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.

**N-3.4** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to those of its
officers, employees, professional advisers and group companies who need to know
it for the Permitted Purpose, provided that the Recipient ensures each such
person is bound by obligations of confidence no less onerous than those in this
Agreement and remains liable for any breach by them.

## N-4 Compelled disclosure

**N-4.1** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent
required by law, by a court of competent jurisdiction or by a regulatory
authority, provided that, to the extent lawful and practicable, the Recipient
gives the Discloser prior written notice sufficient to allow the Discloser to
seek protective measures.

## N-5 Term and survival

**N-5.1** This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for a
period of 2 years, unless terminated earlier by either party on 30 days' written
notice.

**N-5.2** The obligations of confidence in N-3 survive expiry or termination of
this Agreement and continue for a period of 3 years from the date of expiry or
termination.

**N-5.3** By way of exception to N-5.2, the obligations of confidence in respect
of Confidential Information which constitutes a trade secret continue for so long
as that information remains a trade secret.

**N-5.4** By way of exception to N-5.2, the obligations of confidence in respect
of personal data continue for so long as the Recipient holds that personal data.

## N-6 Return and destruction

**N-6.1** On the written request of the Discloser, the Recipient will promptly
return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession and, if
requested, certify in writing that it has done so.

**N-6.2** The Recipient may retain one copy of the Confidential Information to
the extent required by law or by its internal record-keeping or backup policies,
provided that the retained copy remains subject to this Agreement.

## N-7 No licence, no warranty

**N-7.1** No licence of any intellectual property right is granted by this
Agreement.

**N-7.2** The Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Discloser gives no
warranty as to its accuracy or completeness.

## N-8 Remedies

**N-8.1** Each party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy
for a breach of this Agreement and that the Discloser is entitled to seek
injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

## N-9 General

**N-9.1** This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales and the
parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

**N-9.2** No variation of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed
by both parties.

**N-9.3** This Agreement does not create any partnership, agency or joint venture
between the parties, and neither party is obliged to enter into any further
agreement.
