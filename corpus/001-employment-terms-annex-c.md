# Annex C — Working Time, Overtime and Leave

## C-1 Purpose and interpretation

**C-1.1** This Annex forms part of the Employment Terms and governs the
calculation of working time, overtime premia and annual leave accrual for all
Employees engaged under a contract of employment with the Company.

**C-1.2** This Annex is to be read so as to give effect to the Company's
obligations under applicable working time legislation. Where any provision of
this Annex would produce a result less favourable to the Employee than the
statutory minimum, the statutory minimum prevails and this Annex is to be read
as modified accordingly.

**C-1.3** Headings are for convenience only and do not affect construction.

## C-2 Definitions

**C-2.1** "Base Hourly Rate" means the Employee's annual base salary divided by
2,080.

**C-2.2** "Payroll Week" means the period of seven consecutive days commencing
at 00:00 on Monday and ending at 23:59:59 on the following Sunday.

**C-2.3** "Night Hours" means hours worked between 22:00 on any day and 06:00 on
the following day.

**C-2.4** "Grade" means the Employee's job grade as recorded in the Company's
human resources system of record at the first day of the Payroll Week in
question.

**C-2.5** "Critical Incident Response" means work performed in response to an
incident classified as Severity 1 under the Company's incident management
standard, during the period from declaration of the incident until its formal
closure.

**C-2.6** "Gazetted Public Holiday" means a day designated as a bank or public
holiday in the Employee's principal place of work.

## C-3 Ordinary hours

**C-3.1** The ordinary working week is 40 hours.

## C-4 Overtime — general entitlement

**C-4.1** An Employee is entitled to an overtime payment in respect of each hour
worked in excess of 40 hours in a Payroll Week, calculated at 1.25 times the
Base Hourly Rate.

**C-4.2** By way of exception to C-4.1, each hour worked in excess of 48 hours in
a Payroll Week is calculated at 1.5 times the Base Hourly Rate.

## C-5 Overtime — grade exceptions

**C-5.1** By way of exception to C-4, an Employee of Grade 5 or above is not
entitled to an overtime payment. Such an Employee accrues time off in lieu at
the rate of one hour for each hour worked in excess of 40 hours in a Payroll
Week.

**C-5.2** Notwithstanding C-5.1, an Employee of Grade 5 or above is entitled to
an overtime payment calculated in accordance with C-4 in respect of hours worked
on Critical Incident Response.

## C-6 Night work premium

**C-6.1** A premium of 0.15 times the Base Hourly Rate is payable in respect of
each Night Hour worked. This premium is payable in addition to, and not in
substitution for, any amount payable under C-4, C-5.2 or C-7.

**C-6.2** By way of exception to C-6.1, the night premium is not payable in
respect of an Employee who receives a standing shift allowance under their
individual contract of employment.

## C-7 Public holidays

**C-7.1** Each hour worked on a Gazetted Public Holiday is calculated at 2.0
times the Base Hourly Rate. This rate is payable in substitution for, and not in
addition to, any amount otherwise payable under C-4.

**C-7.2** By way of exception to C-7.1, where hours worked on a Gazetted Public
Holiday are also hours worked in excess of 48 hours in the Payroll Week, the
applicable rate is the greater of (a) 2.0 times the Base Hourly Rate and (b) the
rate determined under C-4.2 plus 0.25.

## C-8 Precedence

**C-8.1** Where two or more provisions of C-4, C-5 or C-7 would apply to the same
hour, only the single highest applicable multiplier applies to that hour.

**C-8.2** C-8.1 does not apply to the night premium under C-6, which is
cumulative with any multiplier determined under C-4, C-5.2 or C-7.

## C-9 Annual leave accrual

**C-9.1** An Employee accrues annual leave at the rate of 2.0 days for each
completed calendar month of service.

**C-9.2** Where an Employee commences or terminates employment part-way through a
calendar month, accrual in respect of that month is pro-rated by reference to
the number of days of service in that month as a proportion of the number of
days in that month, rounded to the nearest half day, with an exact quarter
rounded upwards.

**C-9.3** An Employee may carry forward to the next leave year a maximum of 5
accrued but untaken days.

**C-9.4** By way of exception to C-9.3, an Employee who was prevented from taking
accrued leave by reason of long-term sickness absence may carry forward the
whole of their accrued but untaken balance.

**C-9.5** Accrued but untaken leave in excess of the amount permitted to be
carried forward is forfeited at the end of the leave year and is not payable on
termination.

## C-10 Variation

**C-10.1** The Company may vary this Annex by giving not less than one month's
written notice to affected Employees, save that no variation may reduce an
entitlement already accrued at the date the notice takes effect.
