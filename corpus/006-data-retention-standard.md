# Data Retention and Deletion Standard

## R-1 Purpose

**R-1.1** This Standard specifies the periods for which the Company retains
records and the basis on which records are deleted. It applies to all records
held by the Company in any form.

**R-1.2** Retention is a balance between the Company's legitimate need to hold
information and the principle that personal data must not be kept longer than is
necessary. Where this Standard specifies a maximum period, business units are
expected to delete earlier where the purpose for which the record was collected
has been achieved.

## R-2 Definitions

**R-2.1** "Data Class" means the classification assigned to a record under the
Company's information classification policy, being one of: Employee Record,
Customer Contract, Financial Record, Marketing Contact, Security Log, or
Candidate Record.

**R-2.2** "Retention Trigger" means the event from which the retention period is
measured, as specified in R-3.

**R-2.3** "Legal Hold" means a documented instruction issued by the General
Counsel or their delegate to preserve specified records in connection with
actual or reasonably anticipated litigation, investigation or regulatory
proceedings.

**R-2.4** "Deletion" means the destruction of a record such that it cannot be
reconstructed by ordinary means, including deletion from backups at the
expiry of the backup rotation cycle.

## R-3 Retention periods

**R-3.1** Records are retained for the following periods measured from the
applicable Retention Trigger:

  (a) Employee Record: 6 years from the end of employment;
  (b) Customer Contract: 7 years from the expiry or termination of the contract;
  (c) Financial Record: 7 years from the end of the financial year to which it
      relates;
  (d) Marketing Contact: 24 months from the later of the date of collection and
      the date of the most recent engagement by the contact;
  (e) Security Log: 13 months from the date of the logged event;
  (f) Candidate Record: 12 months from the date on which the recruitment process
      concluded.

**R-3.2** By way of exception to R-3.1(f), where the candidate has given consent
to be retained on a talent pool, the Candidate Record is retained for 24 months
from the date of consent, and the period restarts on each renewal of consent.

**R-3.3** By way of exception to R-3.1(a), records required to be retained for a
longer period under pensions or payroll legislation are retained for the period
so required.

## R-4 Legal Hold

**R-4.1** Notwithstanding R-3, a record subject to a Legal Hold must not be
deleted while the Legal Hold remains in force, irrespective of the expiry of the
applicable retention period.

**R-4.2** A Legal Hold takes precedence over any request for erasure made by a
data subject, to the extent permitted by applicable data protection law.

**R-4.3** On release of a Legal Hold, the record is deleted within 30 days if its
retention period has already expired, and otherwise on the ordinary expiry of
its retention period.

## R-5 Erasure requests

**R-5.1** Where a data subject makes a valid request for erasure, the Company will
delete the relevant personal data within 30 days of verifying the request.

**R-5.2** By way of exception to R-5.1, the Company will not delete personal data
where it is required to retain it to comply with a legal obligation, for the
establishment or exercise of legal claims, or where a Legal Hold applies.

## R-6 Deletion of backups

**R-6.1** Records deleted from production systems are deleted from backups at the
expiry of the backup rotation cycle, which must not exceed 90 days.

## R-7 Accountability

**R-7.1** Each business unit is accountable for applying this Standard to the
records it holds. The Privacy Office maintains the record of processing and
reviews compliance annually.

**R-7.2** Any departure from this Standard requires the written approval of the
Privacy Office and is recorded in the exceptions register.
