About oneSaaS: Standard Contract Template

What is oneSaaS?

oneSaaS is a crowd-sourced, open-source standard contract template for Software-as-a-Service or Cloud Services agreements. It is freely available to download and use by anyone.

Does it cost anything to use oneSaaS?

No, oneSaaS is entirely free and always will be. It is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Why use oneSaaS?

Starting from the same standard contract template saves time, money, and effort. It eliminates the battle of negotiating bespoke terms and ensures consistency, enabling businesses to finalize agreements faster and allowing legal teams to focus on strategic initiatives.

When should I use oneSaaS?

oneSaaS is designed for typical SaaS agreements where organizations require a standardized approach to contracting. For highly specialized or complex agreements, consult a lawyer to determine if oneSaaS is suitable.

Who developed it?

oneSaaS was collaboratively developed by leading law firms and in-house legal teams worldwide. No single firm or company is responsible for the development, and participating contributors volunteered their time without providing formal legal advice.Read White Paper

Can I add my company branding to oneSaaS?

No. Keeping branding neutral maintains oneSaaS as an industry standard. This neutrality reduces friction and speeds up negotiations by leveraging its status as a market standard. You may however use your own Order Form alongside oneSaaS which you may brand freely.

Why does oneSaaS not include some of the clauses I’m used to seeing?

oneSaaS focuses on key contractual terms without unnecessary or overly complex provisions. For details on excluded clauses and their rationale, refer to our Graveyard Document.

Can I customize the oneSaaS template?

Yes. You may customize certain sections, add Addendums or include additional provisions in a separate addendum. However, extensive customization undermines the goal of standardization so please ensure you keep modifications to a minimum.

Which jurisdictions does oneSaaS work in?

oneSaaS was developed with global use in mind. However, if in doubt, please seek legal advice to ensure it aligns with local laws and regulations in your jurisdiction.

Can I integrate the terms of oneSaaS into my agreements?

Yes. The terms can be incorporated into your existing Order Forms or other agreements by reference or used as a standalone agreement.

Can I redline the oneSaaS template?

No. Changes should be made using the Special Provisions section in the Key Terms or by including Supplementary Documentation. Allowing redlines would create unnecessary friction, undermining the goal of standardization.
