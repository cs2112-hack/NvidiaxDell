# ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT EXPENSE POLICY

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy governs all expenditures charged to an Engineering cost center. It was
adopted following the Q4 2025 cloud cost review.

## 2. Approval

2.1 All expenses over two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500.00) require Director
approval.

2.2 Expenses of two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500.00) or less require Manager
approval.

2.3 Any new third-party software subscription, at any amount, additionally requires
review by the Platform Architecture group before purchase.

## 3. Cloud Infrastructure

3.1 A new cloud account or project must be tagged to a cost center at creation.

3.2 Any single workload projected to exceed ten thousand dollars ($10,000.00) per month
requires a written cost review filed with the Director of Platform Engineering before
the workload is deployed.

## 4. Hardware

4.1 Standard-issue laptops are refreshed every three (3) years. An out-of-cycle
replacement requires Manager approval and a note of the reason.
