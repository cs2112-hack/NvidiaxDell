# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into as of May 4, 2026 between Acme
Cloud Systems, Inc. and Vertex Analytics Ltd. (each a "party").

## 1. Purpose

1.1 The parties wish to explore a potential commercial relationship concerning data
analytics integration (the "Purpose") and may disclose confidential information to each
other for that Purpose.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to
the other, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would
understand to be confidential in the circumstances of disclosure.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes
publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement, (b) the receiving party
lawfully possessed before disclosure, (c) the receiving party develops independently
without use of or reference to the disclosing party's Confidential Information, or
(d) a third party lawfully discloses to the receiving party without restriction.

## 3. Obligations

3.1 The receiving party shall use the disclosing party's Confidential Information only
for the Purpose, shall protect it with at least reasonable care, and shall disclose it
only to its employees, affiliates, and professional advisers who need it for the
Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than these.

3.2 If the receiving party is compelled by law or court order to disclose Confidential
Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the disclosing party prompt
notice and reasonable cooperation to seek protective treatment.

## 4. Term and Survival

4.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date first written above and continues for two
(2) years, after which either party may stop disclosing Confidential Information.

4.2 The obligations in Section 3 continue for five (5) years after the date of each
disclosure. Obligations with respect to information that constitutes a trade secret
continue for so long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 5. Return or Destruction

5.1 On written request, the receiving party shall return or destroy the disclosing
party's Confidential Information within thirty (30) days, except for one archival copy
retained solely to evidence compliance and for copies in routine backups that are
deleted in the ordinary course.

## 6. No Other Rights

6.1 No license, ownership interest, or obligation to enter into any further agreement
is created by this Agreement. All Confidential Information is provided "as is," without
warranty of accuracy or completeness.

## 7. General

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Neither party may
assign it without the other's written consent.

7.2 The parties acknowledge that damages may be an inadequate remedy for breach and
that the disclosing party may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.
