# CORPORATE EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to all business expenses incurred by employees and contingent
workers of Acme Cloud Systems, Inc. and its US subsidiaries.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

2.1 Every business expense requires approval at or above the level shown below, based
on the total amount of the expense or the total value of the commitment:

| Expense amount | Required approval level |
|---|---|
| $1,000.00 or less | Manager |
| More than $1,000.00 up to and including $5,000.00 | Director |
| More than $5,000.00 up to and including $25,000.00 | Vice President |
| More than $25,000.00 | Chief Financial Officer |

2.2 An expense may not be split into multiple submissions to fall below a threshold.
Recurring commitments are approved at the level determined by the total annual value.

2.3 Self-approval is not permitted at any amount.

## 3. Documentation

3.1 An itemized receipt is required for any single expense over seventy-five dollars
($75.00).

3.2 Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at seventy cents ($0.70) per mile.

3.3 Air travel in a class above economy requires Vice President approval regardless of
fare, except that premium economy is permitted without additional approval for
scheduled flights of more than six (6) hours.

## 4. Non-Reimbursable Items

4.1 The following are not reimbursable: personal entertainment, traffic and parking
fines, airline change fees incurred for personal convenience, and alcohol in excess of
two (2) drinks per person at a business meal.
