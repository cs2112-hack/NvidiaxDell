# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2026
(the "Effective Date") between Acme Cloud Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation
("Acme"), and Northwind Logistics LLC, a Washington limited liability company
("Customer").

## 1. Structure of the Agreement

1.1 This Agreement governs Customer's purchase and use of the Services. The specific
Services, quantities, fees, and term are set out in one or more ordering documents
signed by both parties (each, an "Order Form").

1.2 Exhibit A (Service Level Agreement) is incorporated by reference.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twenty-four (24)
months, and renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless
either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the
end of the then-current period.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Customer shall pay the fees stated in the applicable Order Form ("Fees").

3.2 Acme shall invoice monthly in arrears. Invoices are due net thirty (30) days from
the invoice date.

3.3 Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at one and one-half percent
(1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3.4 Customer may withhold payment of a disputed invoice amount if Customer notifies
Acme of the dispute in writing within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date.

## 4. Termination

4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days'
prior written notice, effective at the end of the then-current billing month.

4.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other party materially
breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after
written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail.

4.3 Acme may suspend the Services immediately if Customer's account is more than
forty-five (45) days past due and Acme has given at least ten (10) days' prior written
notice of the delinquency.

## 5. Customer Data

5.1 Customer retains all right, title, and interest in Customer Data. Processing of
personal data is governed by the Data Processing Addendum.

5.2 On termination, Acme shall make Customer Data available for export for thirty (30)
days and shall delete it within ninety (90) days after the export window closes.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL,
OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS OR LOST REVENUE, WHETHER OR NOT ADVISED OF
THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

9.2 Except as provided in Sections 9.3 and 9.4, each party's aggregate liability
arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid or
payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the first event giving rise to
the claim.

9.3 For Excluded Claims, each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed two hundred
percent (200%) of the total Fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months
preceding the first event giving rise to the claim. "Excluded Claims" means claims
arising from (a) breach of Section 11 (Confidentiality) and (b) a party's indemnity
obligations for third-party intellectual property infringement under Section 10.

9.4 The limitations in Sections 9.2 and 9.3 do not apply to (a) a party's gross
negligence or willful misconduct, (b) Customer's obligation to pay Fees, or (c) either
party's liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence. Liability for
those matters is unlimited.

## 10. Indemnity

10.1 Acme shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the
Services infringe that third party's intellectual property rights, and shall pay
damages finally awarded, provided Customer notifies Acme in writing within thirty (30)
days of becoming aware of the claim and gives Acme sole control of the defense.

## 11. Confidentiality

11.1 Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information using at least the
degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of like importance, and no
less than reasonable care.

11.2 The obligations in Section 11.1 continue for three (3) years after the date of
disclosure, except that obligations with respect to information that qualifies as a
trade secret continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

11.3 "Confidential Information" means information disclosed by a party that is
designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

## 12. Representations and Warranties

12.1 Acme warrants that it will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike
manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and that it will use
commercially reasonable efforts to correct any material non-conformity reported by
Customer.

12.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS"
AND ACME DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE IMPLIED
WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 13. Governing Law and Venue

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, excluding its
conflict-of-laws rules. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state
and federal courts located in San Francisco County, California.

## 14. General

14.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written
consent, except to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of substantially
all of its assets.

14.2 This Agreement, together with its exhibits and all Order Forms, is the entire
agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior proposals and understandings.

14.3 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an
Order Form, the Order Form controls, but solely with respect to the subject matter
expressly addressed in that Order Form. In the event of a conflict between this
Agreement and an exhibit, this Agreement controls.

14.4 If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, that provision shall be
modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining
provisions shall remain in full force.

## 15. Force Majeure

15.1 Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform (other than a failure
to pay) caused by an event beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war,
terrorism, civil unrest, epidemic, labor dispute, or failure of a public utility or
public telecommunications network, provided the affected party gives prompt notice and
uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.
