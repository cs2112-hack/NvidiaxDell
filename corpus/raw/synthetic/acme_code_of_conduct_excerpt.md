# CODE OF BUSINESS CONDUCT (EXCERPT)

## 1. Our Commitment

1.1 We act honestly and in good faith in our dealings with customers, partners,
colleagues, and regulators. Where this Code and a local practice appear to differ, we
apply the higher standard.

## 2. Conflicts of Interest

2.1 A conflict of interest arises where your personal interests could reasonably be
seen to interfere with your judgment on Acme's behalf. You are expected to disclose any
such situation promptly to your manager and to the Legal team, and to remove yourself
from decisions where your objectivity could reasonably be questioned.

2.2 Gifts and entertainment should be modest, infrequent, customary for the business
context, and never offered or accepted to influence a decision. When in doubt, decline
and ask.

## 3. Fair Dealing

3.1 We compete on the merits of our products. We do not disparage competitors, misuse
confidential information belonging to others, or make claims about our services that we
cannot substantiate.

3.2 We do not discuss pricing, market allocation, or bidding intentions with
competitors under any circumstances.

## 4. Speaking Up

4.1 If you believe this Code, the law, or an Acme policy has been or may be violated,
raise it — with your manager, with the Legal team, or anonymously through the ethics
line. Reports are handled confidentially to the extent consistent with a thorough
review.

4.2 Acme does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good
faith or who participates in a review. Retaliation is itself a violation of this Code.

## 5. Using Judgment

5.1 This Code cannot anticipate every situation. Where it does not give a clear answer,
ask whether the action is lawful, whether it is consistent with our commitments to
customers, and whether you would be comfortable explaining it to a colleague or to a
regulator. If any answer is no, or unclear, stop and seek guidance.
