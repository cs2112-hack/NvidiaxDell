# ORDER FORM NO. 1 — ENTERPRISE TIER

Issued under and governed by the Master Services Agreement dated January 1, 2026
between Acme Cloud Systems, Inc. and Northwind Logistics LLC (the "MSA").
Capitalized terms have the meanings given in the MSA.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Order Form effective date | July 1, 2026 |
| Order term | 12 months |
| Service | Acme Cloud Platform — Enterprise Tier |
| Annual Fees | $240,000 (USD) |
| Billing | Monthly in arrears, $20,000 per month |
| Payment terms | Net thirty (30) days |

## 1. Services

Acme shall provide the Acme Cloud Platform at the Enterprise Tier, including the
enterprise support response times set out in Section 3.

## 3. Support Response Times

Acme shall provide an initial response to a Severity 1 ticket within one (1) hour, to a
Severity 2 ticket within four (4) business hours, and to a Severity 3 ticket within
two (2) business days.

## 4. Enterprise Service Levels

4.1 Acme will make the Services available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least
99.9% during each calendar month.

4.2 The Service Credit table in Section A.3 of Exhibit A to the MSA is replaced, for
the Services ordered under this Order Form, with the following:

| Monthly Uptime Percentage | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| Less than 99.9% but equal to or greater than 99.0% | 15% |
| Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0% | 30% |
| Less than 95.0% | 100% |

4.3 The credit request procedure, the 100% monthly cap, and the exclusive-remedy
provision in Section A.4 of Exhibit A continue to apply unchanged.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1 Notwithstanding Section 9.2 of the MSA, each party's aggregate liability arising
out of or related to the Services ordered under this Order Form shall not exceed three
hundred percent (300%) of the Annual Fees stated above.

5.2 Notwithstanding Section 9.3 of the MSA, each party's aggregate liability for
Excluded Claims shall not exceed five hundred percent (500%) of the Annual Fees stated
above.

5.3 Section 9.4 of the MSA (unlimited liability for gross negligence, willful
misconduct, payment obligations, and death or personal injury) is unchanged.

## 6. Precedence

6.1 This Order Form is subject to Section 14.3 of the MSA. To the extent of a conflict
between this Order Form and the MSA or Exhibit A, this Order Form controls with
respect to the subject matter expressly addressed above.
