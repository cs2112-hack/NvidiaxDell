# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## E-1 Scope and purpose

**E-1.1** This Policy governs the reimbursement of expenses properly incurred by
Employees and Contingent Workers in the course of Company business.

**E-1.2** This Policy is not intended to describe every circumstance in which an
expense may be incurred. Where this Policy does not address a circumstance, the
Employee is expected to exercise judgement consistent with the Company's values
and to seek guidance from their line manager before incurring the expense.

**E-1.3** Reimbursement under this Policy does not of itself determine the tax
treatment of a payment. Employees remain responsible for their own personal tax
affairs.

## E-2 Definitions

**E-2.1** "City Tier" means the tier assigned to the city in which the
expenditure is incurred, as published in the Finance intranet schedule, being
Tier 1, Tier 2 or Tier 3.

**E-2.2** "Client-Billable Travel" means travel which is recoverable from a
client under the terms of an executed statement of work.

**E-2.3** "Travel Day" means each calendar day, or part of a calendar day, on
which the Employee is away from their principal place of work overnight on
Company business. The day of departure and the day of return are each a Travel
Day.

**E-2.4** "Pre-Approval" means written approval obtained before the expense is
incurred from a person holding delegated financial authority at or above the
threshold of the expense.

## E-3 Meal per diem

**E-3.1** An Employee is entitled to reimbursement of meal expenses on each
Travel Day up to the following amounts:

  (a) Tier 1 city: £75 per Travel Day;
  (b) Tier 2 city: £55 per Travel Day;
  (c) Tier 3 city: £40 per Travel Day.

**E-3.2** By way of exception to E-3.1, where the travel is Client-Billable
Travel, the applicable per diem is the amount specified in the relevant
statement of work, or if none is specified, 1.5 times the amount under E-3.1.

**E-3.3** By way of exception to E-3.1 and E-3.2, where Pre-Approval has been
obtained for a specified higher amount, that amount applies.

**E-3.4** Where a meal is provided at no cost to the Employee as part of a
conference, client event or airline service, the per diem for that Travel Day is
reduced by £15 in respect of each such meal.

**E-3.5** No per diem is payable in respect of a Travel Day on which the Employee
does not incur any meal expense.

## E-4 Accommodation

**E-4.1** Accommodation is reimbursable at actual cost up to £220 per night in a
Tier 1 city, £160 per night in a Tier 2 city and £120 per night in a Tier 3
city.

**E-4.2** By way of exception to E-4.1, where the Employee provides evidence that
no compliant accommodation was available within the applicable cap at the time
of booking, the actual cost is reimbursable up to 1.4 times the applicable cap.

**E-4.3** The caps in E-4.1 are inclusive of local taxes and of any mandatory
resort or city charge, and exclusive of the cost of parking.

## E-5 Ground transport

**E-5.1** Standard class rail travel is reimbursable at actual cost.

**E-5.2** First class rail travel is reimbursable only where the ticket cost does
not exceed the standard class fare for the same journey booked on the day of
travel, or where Pre-Approval has been obtained.

**E-5.3** Private hire and taxi journeys are reimbursable at actual cost up to
£60 per journey, save that a journey to or from an airport before 06:00 or after
22:00 is reimbursable at actual cost without limit.

## E-6 Submission and time limits

**E-6.1** A claim must be submitted within 60 days after the date on which the
expense was incurred.

**E-6.2** By way of exception to E-6.1, a claim submitted after 60 days but
within 120 days may be reimbursed where the Employee's line manager certifies
that the delay was for good reason.

**E-6.3** No claim submitted more than 120 days after the expense was incurred
will be reimbursed in any circumstances.

## E-7 Relationship with other documents

**E-7.1** Nothing in this Policy affects an Employee's entitlements under Annex C
of the Employment Terms.

**E-7.2** Where an Employee incurs expenses while engaged on Critical Incident
Response within the meaning of Annex C, paragraph C-2.5, the per diem under E-3.1
is increased by £20 per Travel Day and Pre-Approval is not required for that
increase.

## E-8 Consequences of misuse

**E-8.1** The submission of a claim known by the Employee to be false is a
disciplinary matter and may constitute gross misconduct. The Company reserves all
rights in respect of such conduct, including the recovery of sums paid.
