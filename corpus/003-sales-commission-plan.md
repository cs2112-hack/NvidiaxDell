# Sales Commission Plan — FY26

## S-1 Introduction

**S-1.1** This Plan sets out the basis on which commission is earned by Employees
in a quota-bearing sales role during the financial year commencing 1 October 2025
and ending 30 September 2026 (the "Plan Year").

**S-1.2** This Plan is discretionary as to its continuation in future years. No
Employee acquires a contractual right to participate in any successor plan.

**S-1.3** Commission is a variable element of remuneration and is not
pensionable.

## S-2 Definitions

**S-2.1** "Quota" means the annual revenue target assigned to the Employee in
writing at the start of the Plan Year, or on joining if later.

**S-2.2** "Attainment" means Net Booked Revenue expressed as a percentage of
Quota.

**S-2.3** "Net Booked Revenue" means the aggregate annual contract value of
contracts signed by the Employee during the Plan Year, less the annual contract
value of any Churned Contract.

**S-2.4** "Churned Contract" means a contract which is terminated by the
customer, or which is not renewed at its first renewal date, in either case
within 180 days after its signature date.

**S-2.5** "Base Commission Rate" means 8.0% of Net Booked Revenue.

**S-2.6** "New Logo" means a customer which had no contract with the Company at
any time in the 24 months preceding the signature date.

## S-3 Commission calculation

**S-3.1** Commission is calculated as Net Booked Revenue multiplied by the Base
Commission Rate, multiplied by the accelerator determined under S-4.

## S-4 Accelerators

**S-4.1** The accelerator is determined by Attainment as follows:

  (a) Attainment of less than 50%: accelerator of 0.0;
  (b) Attainment of 50% or more but less than 100%: accelerator of 1.0;
  (c) Attainment of 100% or more but less than 150%: accelerator of 1.5;
  (d) Attainment of 150% or more: accelerator of 2.0.

**S-4.2** The accelerator determined under S-4.1 applies to the whole of Net
Booked Revenue and not only to the portion above the relevant threshold.

**S-4.3** By way of exception to S-4.1, where more than 60% of Net Booked Revenue
is attributable to New Logo customers, the accelerator determined under S-4.1 is
increased by 0.25.

**S-4.4** By way of exception to S-4.1 and S-4.3, the accelerator applicable to an
Employee who commenced employment after the start of the Plan Year is capped at
1.5 for the Plan Year in which they commenced.

## S-5 Caps

**S-5.1** Total commission payable to an Employee in respect of the Plan Year
must not exceed 250% of the Employee's annual base salary.

**S-5.2** The cap in S-5.1 is applied after the accelerator under S-4 and after
any adjustment under S-6.

**S-5.3** By way of exception to S-5.1, where the Employee's Attainment exceeds
200%, the cap is 400% of annual base salary.

## S-6 Quota relief

**S-6.1** Where an Employee is absent on statutory parental leave, adoption leave
or long-term sickness absence for a continuous period of 30 days or more, the
Employee's Quota is reduced by 1/365th of the annual Quota for each day of such
absence.

**S-6.2** Quota relief under S-6.1 is applied before Attainment is calculated.

## S-7 Clawback

**S-7.1** Where commission has been paid in respect of a contract which
subsequently becomes a Churned Contract, the Company may recover the commission
attributable to that contract by deduction from future commission payments or,
where no such payments fall due, as a debt.

**S-7.2** By way of exception to S-7.1, no recovery will be made where the
contract was terminated by the customer by reason of a material breach by the
Company.

**S-7.3** No recovery will be made more than 24 months after the date on which
the commission was paid.

## S-8 Leavers

**S-8.1** An Employee who ceases employment is entitled to commission in respect
of contracts signed on or before their last day of employment, payable in the
ordinary payment cycle.

**S-8.2** By way of exception to S-8.1, an Employee whose employment is terminated
for gross misconduct forfeits any commission not yet paid at the date of
termination.

## S-9 Disputes

**S-9.1** Any question as to the interpretation or operation of this Plan will be
determined by the Chief Revenue Officer, whose determination is final. This
paragraph does not exclude any statutory right of the Employee.
