# Employee Referral Bonus Scheme

## B-1 Purpose

**B-1.1** This Scheme sets out the bonus payable to an Employee who refers a
candidate whom the Company subsequently engages.

## B-2 Definitions

**B-2.1** "Referred Hire" means a candidate introduced by an Employee through
the Company's referral portal who is subsequently engaged under a contract of
employment.

**B-2.2** "Qualifying Period" means the period of 180 days beginning on the
Referred Hire's first day of employment.

**B-2.3** "Hard-to-Fill Role" means a role designated as such in the Company's
approved requisition at the date the referral is submitted.

## B-3 Bonus entitlement

**B-3.1** An Employee who makes a referral resulting in a Referred Hire is
entitled to a bonus of £1,000.

**B-3.2** By way of exception to B-3.1, where the Referred Hire is engaged at
Grade 5 or above, the bonus is £2,500.

**B-3.3** By way of exception to B-3.1 and B-3.2, where the role is a
Hard-to-Fill Role, the bonus is £4,000.

**B-3.4** Notwithstanding B-3.3, no bonus is payable where the candidate was
introduced to the Company by a recruitment agency before the referral was
submitted.

**B-3.5** By way of exception to B-3.4, where the agency introduction occurred
more than 12 months before the referral was submitted, the bonus is payable and
is calculated under B-3.1 to B-3.3.

## B-4 Payment and forfeiture

**B-4.1** The bonus is payable in the payroll cycle following the expiry of the
Qualifying Period.

**B-4.2** No bonus is payable where the Referred Hire ceases employment before
the expiry of the Qualifying Period.

**B-4.3** By way of exception to B-4.2, where the Referred Hire ceases
employment by reason of redundancy, the bonus remains payable.

## B-5 Aggregate limit

**B-5.1** The total bonus payable to an Employee under this Scheme in any
financial year must not exceed £10,000.

**B-5.2** The limit in B-5.1 is applied after any adjustment under B-3 and B-4.

## B-6 General

**B-6.1** This Scheme is discretionary and may be withdrawn by the Company at
any time in respect of referrals not yet submitted.

**B-6.2** Any question as to the interpretation of this Scheme is determined by
the Chief People Officer.
