# Annex C Amendment 1

## A-1 Purpose

**A-1.1** This Amendment varies Annex C of the Employment Terms with effect from
1 January 2026, in accordance with paragraph C-10.1.

## A-2 Revised definitions

**A-2.1** "Base Hourly Rate" means the Employee's annual base salary divided by
2,088.

## A-3 Revised overtime rates

**A-3.1** An Employee is entitled to an overtime payment in respect of each hour
worked in excess of 40 hours in a Payroll Week, calculated at 1.35 times the
Base Hourly Rate.

**A-3.2** By way of exception to C-11.4, the enhanced rate does not apply to
Employees engaged on a fixed-term contract of less than 6 months.

## A-4 Night work

**A-4.1** A premium of 0.20 times the Base Hourly Rate is payable in respect of
each Night Hour worked.
