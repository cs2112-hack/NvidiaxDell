The corpus, as ingested

What does the policy require?

A question about a rule is answered by executing that rule. A question about wording is answered by quoting the clause. Both may appear, and each is labelled with the engine that produced it.

Rules the system can execute

Each scope encodes named clauses and computes a result from facts you supply. Where a clause turns on a judgement the documents do not define, that judgement is an input a person fills in — the rule never decides it.

Documents awaiting a decision

An incoming document becomes a proposal, never a merge. Every blocking conflict needs a person to record how it is resolved and who resolved it before the document can join the corpus.

What has been checked
